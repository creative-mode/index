Frequently asked questions

How does a project start?

With a conversation about the problem. Then we run a diagnosis and present a proposed approach, with scope, timeline and team.

How long does it take?

It depends on the scope. We work in short cycles, so there are deliveries from the first weeks, and the full timeline is set in the diagnosis.

Does our data need to be organised first?

No. Organising your data and making sure it is reliable is part of our AI and Data work.

Do you work with our in house team?

Yes. We integrate the solution into your systems and prepare your team to use and maintain it.

What happens after delivery?

We stay with you: we measure the results and keep improving the solution over time.
